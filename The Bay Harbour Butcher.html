The Code

At the heart of the series is a private moral framework, taught to Dexter Morgan by his adoptive father, Harry. It is a discipline first, and a justification second.

  1. Don't get caught.
    Every other principle serves this one.
  2. Be certain.
    A target must be guilty beyond any reasonable doubt.
  3. Stay disciplined.
    Preparation, patience and a clean procedure.

Why it endures

The series asks the audience to weigh a tidy system against the damage it does to everyone close to it. The Code promises order; the seasons repeatedly show its cost.
